Detailed Description

Simulated oscilloscope for demonstrations and testing.

Four channels: 1 GHz sine 1 GHz sine plus 1.1 - 1.5 GHz ramp 10.3125 Gbps PRBS-31 1.25 Gbps 8B/10B K28.5 D16.2 idle sequence
