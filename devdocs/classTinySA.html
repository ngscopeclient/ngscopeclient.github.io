Detailed Description

Driver for TinySA and TinySA Ultra Spectrum Analizers.

TinySA and TinySA Ultra are hobyist low-cost Spectrum Analizer designed by Erik Kaashoek: https://tinysa.org/ They can be connected to a PC via a USB COM port.
