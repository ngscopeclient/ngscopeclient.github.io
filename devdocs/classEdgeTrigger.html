◆ EdgeType

enum EdgeTrigger::EdgeType

Types of edges to trigger on.

Enumerator
EDGE_RISING 

Low to high transition.

EDGE_FALLING 

High to low transition.

EDGE_ANY 

Either rising or falling transition.

EDGE_ALTERNATING 

Trigger on rising edge, then falling, then rising again, and so on.

Not widely supported, only known to be implemented by a handful of Agilent models
